George Brown College

George Brown College, now known as George Brown Polytechnic, is a post secondary school in Toronto. Its 3 main campuses are Casa Loma, St. James and the Waterfront campus. The school offers certificates, diplomas and degrees. 

I'm (very) biased xD, but I think it's an amazing college and I'm proud to be a student here.

I personally live in Halifax, Nova Scotia. George Brown offers some great online programs like the one I'm in, T-197. These give people outside of Toronto the chance to study at the college without having to move. This has made a huge difference for me.
