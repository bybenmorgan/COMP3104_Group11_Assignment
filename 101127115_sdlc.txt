DevOps in the SDLC

Just because a dev has a feature working on their laptop doesn't mean it's ready to be released. During development and testing, Git tracks the changes, PRs give the team a chance to review them, and CI pipelines can run builds and tests automatically and easily. These all help catch problems before they can reach the users.

DevOps is also super important for deployment and maintenance. Automating the release steps makes deployments way more consistent and reduces the chance that someone could miss a step when doing it manually. When an application is running, monitoring helps the team spot errors and performance issues.
