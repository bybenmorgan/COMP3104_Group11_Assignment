DevOps in the SDLC

Just because a dev has a feature working on their laptop, doesn't mean its ready to be released. During development and testing, Git tracks the changes, PRs, give the team a chance to review them, and CI piplines can run builds and test automatically and easily. These all help catch problems before they can reach the users.

DevOps is also super important for deployment and maintenance. Automating the release steps makes deployments way more consistent and reduces the chance thay someone could miss a step when doing it manually. When an applicaiton is running, monitoring helps the team spot errors and performance issues.
